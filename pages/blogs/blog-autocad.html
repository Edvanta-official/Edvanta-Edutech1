Complete 4-Module Curriculum Breakdown

Module 1: AutoCAD User Interface & 2D Drawing Commands

Master coordinate systems, precision snapping, line/arc/circle drafting commands, modify tools, dynamic blocks, and layer management.

Module 2: Architectural & Civil Blueprint Drafting

Draft detailed residential floor plans, structural wall layouts, doors/windows schedules, roof elevations, and section views.

Module 3: Mechanical Component & Assembly Drawings

Create mechanical part Orthographic views, Isometric projections, dimensioning standards, surface finish symbols, and Bill of Materials (BOM).

Module 4: 3D Solid Modeling, Rendering & Sheet Sets

Extrude 2D sketches into 3D solid components, apply materials/lighting, generate section views, and organize multi-sheet PDF submittals.
